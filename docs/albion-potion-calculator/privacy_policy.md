# Privacy Policy — Albion Craft Calculator

_Last updated: July 13, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Albion Craft Calculator** mobile application ("the App"), available on Android and iOS, handles your information. Our primary goal is to provide a useful tool while maintaining the highest standards of user privacy.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

**Albion Craft Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

The App is designed to run entirely on your local device. We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or any other sensitive personal data. Your crafting preferences, favorite items, language, and settings are stored locally on your device (using the platform's standard local storage) and are never transmitted to our servers.

## 2. Network and External Services

### Market Data Integration
To provide real-time crafting calculations, the App fetches public market data from the **Albion Online Data Project API** (https://www.albion-online-data.com). These requests are anonymous and do not contain any personal or device-identifiable information.

### In-App Purchases
Where premium features are offered, purchases are processed entirely by the platform's app store — **Google Play Billing** on Android and the **Apple App Store** on iOS.
- **Financial Privacy**: All payment processing is handled securely by the respective app store. We do not have access to, nor do we store, your credit card numbers, bank details, or other payment credentials.
- **Entitlement Tracking**: We receive only a purchase/transaction identifier from the store to verify your purchase and unlock premium functionality on your device.

_Note: the iOS version is currently offered free of charge and contains no in-app purchases._

## 3. Advertisements

Advertising differs by platform:

- **Android**: The App displays banner advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads). AdMob may use a device **Advertising ID** to serve ads. On first launch the App presents a **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** consent request in regions where it is required. Google's handling of ad data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users who purchase premium do not see ads.
- **iOS**: The iOS version is **ad-free** and contains no advertising or advertising identifiers.

## 4. No Third-Party Analytics

The App does **not** include third-party analytics or behavior-tracking services (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics). We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.
