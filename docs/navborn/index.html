Privacy Policy

com.greedmitya.navborn

Offline Game Zero Data Collection Updated: Aug 27, 2026

"NavBorn is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. We collect nothing, track nothing, and send nothing. Your grind is yours alone."

1. Data We Collect

None. NavBorn does not collect or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

No Tracking

No advertising ID, no analytics SDKs (Firebase, Meta, Mixpanel), no telemetry.

No Permissions

No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, or your personal files.

2. Local Save Data

Your progress — character level, gear, stash, gold, and settings — is stored in a local file on your device only. It never leaves your device, we cannot access it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.

3. Children & Third Parties

Because the app collects no data from anyone, no information from children or adults is ever gathered. The app is distributed via Google Play; any processing during download is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us. NavBorn integrates no third-party data-collecting SDKs.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA you may access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing on our side to retrieve or erase. To delete all local data, uninstall NavBorn or clear its data in Android Settings.
