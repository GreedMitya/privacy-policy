FAQ

Do I need an account or internet to play?

No account, ever. The whole game is single-player and fully playable offline. An internet connection is only used for the optional global leaderboards ("Ranks") — without it, Ranks just shows your personal bests.

Where is my progress saved?

Your character, gear, stash, and settings are saved locally on your device. The only thing sent to a server is an optional leaderboard score (an anonymous ID, a nickname you choose, and your run results) via Unity Gaming Services.

How do I delete my data?

To erase your local save, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → NavBorn → Storage → Clear data. To delete your leaderboard record from the online ladders, email us your leaderboard nickname and we'll remove it.

I lost an item after dying — is that a bug?

No. NavBorn has a punishing (softcore) death penalty by design: you lose some XP and equipped items have a small chance to be lost forever. Push into the deep carefully.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. NavBorn has no ads, no advertising tracking, and no in-app purchases.

My leaderboard name is showing publicly — how do I change or hide it?

Your Ranks nickname is public, so please don't use personal info. You can change it anytime in the Ranks screen; email us to have your leaderboard record removed entirely.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email senseimitya@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
