FAQ

Is NavBorn online? Do I need an account?

No. NavBorn is a fully offline single-player game. There is no account, no login, and no internet connection required to play.

Where is my progress saved?

Your character, gear, stash, and settings are saved locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → NavBorn → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes your local save.

I lost an item after dying — is that a bug?

No. NavBorn has a punishing (softcore) death penalty by design: you lose some XP and equipped items have a small chance to be lost forever. Push into the deep carefully.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. NavBorn has no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email senseimitya@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
